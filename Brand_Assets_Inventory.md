## Usage Guidelines

### Digital Applications
- Use high-resolution PNG files for web and digital applications
- Maintain proper aspect ratios and scaling
- Ensure adequate contrast for accessibility
- Test logo visibility across different devices and backgrounds

### Print Applications
- Use vector formats when available for scalability
- Maintain color consistency across different printing methods
- Ensure proper bleed areas for printed materials
- Use appropriate paper stocks for professional appearance

### Brand Consistency
- Always use approved logo variations
- Maintain consistent color palette implementation
- Follow typography hierarchy guidelines
- Ensure proper spacing and proportions
