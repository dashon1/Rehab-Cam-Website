## Essential Property Analysis Metrics

### Financial Performance Indicators

#### Return on Investment (ROI) Metrics

**Cash-on-Cash Return**
- Formula: (Annual Cash Flow / Total Cash Invested) × 100
- Target: 6-10% for buy-and-hold properties (market-dependent)
- 10-20% for house flipping projects (varies significantly by market)
- 12-18% for value-add opportunities (highly variable)

**Cap Rate (Capitalization Rate)**
- Formula: (Net Operating Income / Property Value) × 100
- Use: Property valuation and comparison tool
- Target: 4-8% for residential properties
- 6-10% for commercial properties

**Internal Rate of Return (IRR)**
- Formula: Complex calculation accounting for time value of money
- Use: Long-term investment performance measurement
- Target: 8-15% for most real estate investments (varies by market conditions)
- Considers all cash flows over investment period

**Total Return on Investment**
- Formula: (Total Profit + Cash Flow) / Total Investment × 100
- Use: Overall investment performance assessment
- Includes appreciation, cash flow, and tax benefits

#### Cash Flow Analysis

**Gross Rental Income**
- Current rental rates for similar properties
- Seasonal variations and occupancy rates
- Potential rent increases and market trends
- Additional income sources (parking, laundry, storage)

**Operating Expenses**
- Property taxes and insurance costs
- Maintenance and repair reserves
- Property management fees
- Utilities and common area expenses
- Vacancy and credit loss allowances

**Net Operating Income (NOI)**
- Formula: Gross Rental Income - Operating Expenses
- Foundation for cap rate and cash flow calculations
- Excludes financing costs and depreciation

### Market Comparison Metrics

#### Comparable Sales Analysis

**Price per Square Foot**
- Calculate for subject property and comparables
- Adjust for condition, location, and timing
- Identify pricing trends and market position
- Use for ARV estimation and negotiation

**Days on Market**
- Compare subject property to market averages
- Identify market timing and pricing strategy
- Assess property attractiveness and condition
- Predict time to sale or lease

**Price to Rent Ratio**
- Formula: Property Price / Annual Rental Income
- Use: Investment property valuation and comparison
- Target ranges vary by market and property type
- Indicates market affordability and investment potential

#### Market Absorption Analysis

**Inventory Levels**
- Months of inventory available
- Supply and demand balance indicators
- Market speed and competitive positioning
- Future inventory projections

**Sales Velocity**
- Transaction volume trends
- Market activity and liquidity indicators
- Seasonal patterns and cyclical variations
- Economic factor correlations
