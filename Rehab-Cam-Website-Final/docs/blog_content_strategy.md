# Real Estate Tech Blog Content Strategy

## Executive Summary
This content strategy focuses on establishing thought leadership in real estate technology while driving qualified leads for our rehab estimation platform. Target audience includes real estate investors, house flippers, contractors, and real estate agents.

---

## Target Audience Segments

### Primary Audiences
1. **Real Estate Investors** (35-55 years, $75K+ income)
   - Pain points: Accurate rehab costs, market analysis, ROI calculations
   - Goals: Maximize profits, minimize risks, scale investments

2. **House Flippers** (30-50 years, $50K+ income)
   - Pain points: Project timeline management, contractor costs, market timing
   - Goals: Complete profitable flips quickly, build contractor networks

3. **Real Estate Agents** (25-45 years, commission-based income)
   - Pain points: Client education, property valuation, market insights
   - Goals: Close more deals, provide better service, increase commissions

### Secondary Audiences
4. **Contractors & Construction Professionals**
5. **Real Estate Rehabbers & Wholesalers**

## Content Production Workflow

### Content Calendar Management
- **Monthly Planning Sessions** (first week of each month)

- **Quarterly Strategy Reviews** (end of each quarter)

1. **Research Phase** (2-3 days)
   - Keyword research
   - Competitor analysis
   - Industry trend identification
   - Subject matter expert consultation

2. **Writing Phase** (3-5 days)

   - SEO optimization

3. **Production Phase** (2-3 days)
   - Graphics and image creation
   - Video content development
   - Social media adaptations
   - Email sequence creation

4. **Publishing Phase** (1 day)

   - SEO implementation
   - Social media scheduling
   - Distribution execution
