### Content Performance Optimization

#### Hashtag Performance Tracking
**Monitor and Track:**
- Engagement rates by hashtag combination
- Reach and impressions by hashtag type
- Click-through rates to website/blog
- Lead generation by content type

**High-Performing Combinations:**
- #RehabECam + #LiveXray + #RealEstateInvesting
- #PropTech + #PropertyAnalysis + #HouseFlipping
- #RealEstateTechnology + #AR + #Innovation

**A/B Testing Strategy:**
- Test 3-4 hashtag combinations per content type
- Track performance over 30-day periods
- Optimize based on engagement and conversion data
- Refresh hashtag strategy quarterly

#### Content Mix Guidelines
**Weekly Distribution:**
- 40% Educational/Industry insights
- 30% Customer success and case studies
- 20% Technology and product focus
- 10% Company news and updates

**Monthly Distribution:**
- 25% Professional posts (industry content)
- 25% Thought leadership articles
- 20% Company updates and announcements
- 20% Engagement posts with questions
- 10% User-generated content and community features

#### Engagement Optimization Tactics

**Call-to-Action Variations:**
- "Comment below with your experience"
- "Share your biggest challenge"
- "What's your strategy for [topic]?"
- "Drop your questions in the comments"
- "Tag a colleague who needs to see this"

**Question Strategies:**
- Start with shocking statistics or facts
- Ask about specific industry challenges
- Request personal experiences and stories
- Create polls with multiple choice options
- Encourage debate and discussion

**Response Strategy:**
- Respond to all comments within 2-4 hours
- Ask follow-up questions to commenters
- Share additional insights and resources
- Direct serious prospects to private messages
- Thank participants and acknowledge contributions
