## 📊 Content Strategy Framework

### The 70/20/10 Rule
- **70% Educational Content:** Industry insights, technology trends, best practices
- **20% Community & Engagement:** Success stories, user content, networking
- **10% Promotional Content:** Product features, company news, CTAs

### Weekly Content Themes
- **Monday:** Market Monday (industry trends and analysis)
- **Tuesday:** Tech Tuesday (PropTech innovations)
- **Wednesday:** Wisdom Wednesday (educational content)
- **Thursday:** Transformation Thursday (success stories)
- **Friday:** Feature Friday (product highlights)
