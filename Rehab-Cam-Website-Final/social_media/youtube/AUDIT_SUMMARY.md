## Major Issues Corrected

### 1. Misleading Financial Claims
**Problem**: Titles promised specific financial outcomes
- "From $50K to $2M in 3 Years" 
- "The $100K Lead Generation System That Actually Works"
- "Market Analysis Mastery - Read Any Market in 15 Minutes"

**Corrections Made**:
- Changed to educational focus titles
- Removed specific dollar amounts and timeframes
- Emphasized learning and strategy over guaranteed results

### 2. Unverifiable Success Statistics
**Problem**: Claims like "87% of agents fail" and specific performance metrics
- "87% of real estate agents fail within their first two years"
- "Agents who follow our systems average 40% more listings"
- "300% increase in social media leads"

**Corrections Made**:
- Replaced with general market observations
- Changed to potential benefits rather than guaranteed results
- Removed specific percentage claims without verification

### 3. False Testimonials and Social Proof
**Problem**: Unverifiable success stories and community claims
- "Our community has generated over $X million"
- "I've helped over 500 real estate professionals"
- "I've analyzed markets in 47 different cities"

**Corrections Made**:
- Changed to educational positioning
- Removed specific client numbers and transaction values
- Focused on practical strategies over personal achievements

### 4. Unrealistic ROI Claims
**Problem**: Specific ROI promises without verification
- "ROI: $10-20K monthly in new business"
- "ROI: 300% increase in social media leads"
- "ROI: Time savings of 15 hours/week"

**Corrections Made**:
- Changed to "Potential benefit" language
- Removed specific dollar amounts and percentages
- Focused on general efficiency improvements

### 5. Overpromising Guarantees
**Problem**: Language suggesting guaranteed results
- "That Actually Works"
- "That's repeatable"
- "The system that's generated over $100 million"

**Corrections Made**:
- Replaced with "can help" and "may improve" language
- Removed guaranteed success language
- Emphasized learning and application
