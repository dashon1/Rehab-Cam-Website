## Video Title Optimization Strategy

### Formula 1: [Number] + [Benefit] + [Keyword]
Examples:
- "5 Lead Generation Scripts That Convert in 2024"
- "10 Tech Tools Every Real Estate Agent Needs"
- "3 Pricing Strategies That Win More Listings"

### Formula 2: [How To] + [Achieve Result] + [Keyword]
Examples:
- "How to Generate 50+ Leads Per Month in Real Estate"

## Description Optimization Framework

### Structure:
1. **Hook** (First 2 sentences): Value proposition + keyword
2. **Benefits List**: 3-5 specific outcomes viewers will get
3. **Timestamps**: For longer videos
4. **Call-to-Action**: Subscribe, download resources
5. **Keywords**: Natural keyword integration
6. **Hashtags**: 3-5 relevant hashtags

## Content Optimization for Real Estate Keywords

### 1. Video Structure
- **Hook**: Use primary keyword in first 15 seconds
- **Introduction**: Include keyword variations naturally
- **Main Content**: LSI keywords throughout
- **Conclusion**: Reinforce primary keyword

### 2. Keyword Density
- Primary keyword: 1-2% density
- Secondary keywords: 0.5-1% density
- Natural integration (avoid keyword stuffing)

### 3. Semantic Keywords
- Real estate agent → realtor, property agent, sales representative
- Lead generation → prospecting, client acquisition, marketing
- Investment → investing, portfolio, wealth building

## YouTube Analytics Keywords

1. **Impressions**: How often video appears in searches
2. **Click-Through Rate**: % of impressions that become views
3. **Watch Time**: Total minutes watched
4. **Audience Retention**: % of video watched
5. **Traffic Sources**: YouTube search vs. suggested vs. external
