## Story Arc (45 seconds - 5:00 minutes)

### The Starting Point (45 seconds - 1:30 minutes)
"Let me paint you the picture. Sarah Martinez was 32, divorced, with two kids, working 60-hour weeks between a retail job and waitressing. Her credit score was 520, she had $50K in consumer debt, and she was living paycheck to paycheck.

But she had something that changed everything: she was desperate for change and willing to do whatever it took. Sound familiar?"

### The Turning Point (1:30 - 2:30 minutes)
"Everything changed when Sarah attended my free real estate workshop. She learned about house hacking - buying a multi-family property, living in one unit, and renting the others to cover the mortgage.

Her first question was: 'But how do I buy a house with terrible credit and no money?' Great question! Here's what we taught her..."

### The Strategy (2:30 - 4:00 minutes)
"Step 1: Credit Repair (Months 1-3)
- Sarah got her free credit report and found 47 errors
- Used dispute process to remove negative items
- Became an authorized user on her sister's credit card
- Result: Credit score jumped from 520 to 680

Step 2: Income Validation (Months 2-4)
- Documented ALL income sources, including tips
- Started a side hustle in real estate virtual assisting
- Built a portfolio of 3 successful transactions

Step 3: Down Payment Strategy (Months 3-5)
- Used FHA loan program (3.5% down)
- Seller concessions covered closing costs
- Her mother gifted $5,000 for down payment"

### The First Deal (4:00 - 4:30 minutes)
"By month 6, Sarah bought a duplex for $180,000. She lived in one side, rented the other for $1,200/month. Her mortgage payment was $1,100. She was cash-flowing positive from day one!"

### The Growth (4:30 - 5:30 minutes)
"Here's where it gets interesting. Sarah used the equity in her first property to buy her next one. By year 2, she had 4 properties. By year 3, 8 properties. Each property added to her net worth and monthly cash flow.

Today, she owns 12 properties, worth $2.1 million, generating $18K monthly in passive income. She's now a licensed real estate agent and helps other single parents achieve financial freedom."

## Key Lessons (5:30 - 6:00 minutes)
"Here's what made Sarah successful:
1. She started with education and a plan
2. She leveraged programs available to first-time buyers
3. She reinvested profits into more properties
4. She never stopped learning and networking
5. She treated it like a business, not a hobby"
