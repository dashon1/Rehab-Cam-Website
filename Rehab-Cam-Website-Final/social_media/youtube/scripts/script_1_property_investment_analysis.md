## Main Content (45 seconds - 3:30 minutes)

### Step 1: Market Analysis (45 seconds - 1:30 minutes)
"Let's start with the foundation: understanding your market. I always begin by analyzing:
- Population growth trends (are people moving TO or FROM your area?)
- Job market stability (check local employment data)
- Rental demand indicators
- New construction permits filed

I use a simple spreadsheet template that you can download in the description - link is below!"

### Step 2: Financial Calculations (1:30 - 2:15 minutes)
"Next, we crunch the numbers using the 1% rule as a quick filter:
- Monthly rent should be 1% of purchase price for cash flow
- Calculate cash-on-cash return (target 8-12%)
- Factor in all costs: mortgage, insurance, maintenance, vacancy

Let me show you my exact formula..." [Show screen recording]

### Step 3: Due Diligence Checklist (2:15 - 2:45 minutes)
"Here's what most beginners miss:
- Property inspection (hire professionals!)
- Comparative Market Analysis (CMA)
- Zoning restrictions and future developments
- Environmental factors and HOA rules"

### Step 4: Exit Strategy Planning (2:45 - 3:15 minutes)
"Every investment needs an exit strategy:
- Hold for rental income
- Fix and flip potential
- 1031 exchange opportunities
- Long-term appreciation zones"

### Step 5: Risk Assessment (3:15 - 3:30 minutes)
"Finally, identify potential risks:
- Market volatility factors
- Property-specific challenges
- Cash flow stability
- Economic indicators"
