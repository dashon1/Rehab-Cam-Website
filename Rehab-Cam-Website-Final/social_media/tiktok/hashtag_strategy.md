## Hashtag Research Tools & Methods

### TikTok-Specific Tools
1. **TikTok Creative Center**: Shows trending hashtags by category
2. **Hashtag Challenge Discovery**: Monitor trending challenges
3. **Competitor Analysis**: See what successful agents use

### General Research Methods
1. **Google Trends**: Compare hashtag popularity
2. **Social Media Management Tools**: Hootsuite, Buffer hashtag analytics
3. **Industry Reports**: NAR, local association data
4. **Local Real Estate Groups**: See what your network uses

## Hashtag Performance Tracking

### Key Metrics to Monitor
- **Reach**: How many unique users saw content
- **Engagement**: Likes, comments, shares per hashtag
- **Click-through**: Profile visits from hashtag clicks
- **Conversion**: Leads generated from hashtag discovery

### Weekly Hashtag Analysis
- Top 5 performing hashtags from your content
- Trending hashtags in your market
- Hashtags used by successful competitors
- Seasonal/timely hashtag opportunities

## Hashtag Mistakes to Avoid

### Common Errors
1. **Using too many hashtags** (stick to 3-5 for TikTok)
2. **Using irrelevant hashtags** (no #Food when posting about houses)
3. **Spammy repetition** (don't use same hashtags every post)
4. **Ignoring trending tags** (missed opportunities)
5. **Not tracking performance** (guessing instead of data-driven)

### Best Practices
- Mix high, medium, and low competition hashtags
- Update hashtag strategy monthly
- A/B test different combinations
- Stay authentic to your brand
- Focus on engagement over pure reach
