### Content Production Quick Reference

#### Video Structure Template
```
0-3s: Hook (Attention-grabbing opening)
3-25s: Content (Core value delivery)
25-30s: Call-to-Action (Next step for viewers)
```

#### Hashtag Formula
```
1 trending hashtag + 2 niche hashtags + 2 local/market hashtags
```

#### Weekly Posting Schedule
- **Monday**: Market update/Educational
- **Tuesday**: Quick tips/Educational  
- **Wednesday**: Behind-the-scenes/Process
- **Thursday**: Trending format/Humor
- **Friday**: Property showcase/Community

### Success Metrics to Track

#### Primary KPIs
- **Engagement Rate**: Target 8%+ (industry average: 3-5%)
- **View Completion Rate**: Target 70%+ completion
- **Profile Visits**: Track week-over-week growth
- **Comment Quality**: Monitor meaningful vs. spam comments

#### Lead Generation Tracking
- Direct messages from TikTok
- Profile click-through to website/contact forms
- Phone calls mentioning "TikTok"
- Email inquiries with "from TikTok" reference

### Troubleshooting Common Issues

#### Low Engagement Solutions
- Improve hook quality (first 3 seconds)
- Add trending audio to existing content
- Increase posting frequency temporarily
- Ask questions in captions to drive comments
- Collaborate with other creators

#### Algorithm Challenges
- Post during peak audience hours
- Engage with comments within 2 hours
- Use trending hashtags strategically
- Create content that encourages shares
- Maintain consistent posting schedule

#### Content Ideas Running Dry
- Review comments for content requests
- Adapt trending formats to real estate
- Share client success stories (with permission)
- Create educational series from blog posts
- Collaborate with other professionals

### Legal & Ethical Considerations

#### Content Guidelines
- Always get permission for client content
- Avoid sharing confidential information
- Include disclaimers for financial advice
- Respect copyright for music and images
- Maintain professional standards in all content

#### Best Practices
- Fact-check all market data before sharing
- Use general examples rather than specific deals
- Include relevant disclaimers where appropriate
- Respect platform community guidelines
- Maintain consistent brand voice and values
