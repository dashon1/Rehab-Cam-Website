# Instagram Stories Templates
## 10 Interactive Stories Templates

### Story 1: AR Feature Tutorial
**Format**: Step-by-step tutorial (7 slides)
**Interactive**: "Swipe up to download" sticker
**Caption**: "See through walls! ✨ AR tutorial inside"

### Story 2: Before/After Reveal
**Format**: Slider reveal with ROI poll
**Interactive**: "Guess the ROI" multiple choice poll
**Caption**: "Guess the ROI on this transformation! 🏠"

### Story 3: Quick Tips Series
**Format**: Educational carousel (5 daily tips)
**Interactive**: "Which tip surprised you?" poll
**Caption**: "5 inspection tips in 5 days! Save this series"

### Story 4: User Success Spotlight
**Format**: Testimonial feature with results
**Interactive**: "Want to be featured?" question sticker
**Caption**: "Real investors, real results! 🎉"

### Story 5: Market Insights
**Format**: Data visualization with charts
**Interactive**: "How's your local market?" poll
**Caption**: "Weekly market intelligence 📊"

### Story 6: Mobile vs Desktop Poll
**Format**: Quick preference poll
**Interactive**: Two-option poll with follow-up questions
**Caption**: "Quick poll for real estate pros! 📱💻"

### Story 7: Behind the Scenes
**Format**: Team culture and development process
**Interactive**: "Which feature are you most excited for?" poll
**Caption**: "Meet the team building your proptech! 👥"

### Story 8: Cost Estimation Challenge
**Format**: Interactive challenge game
**Interactive**: "What's your estimate?" question sticker
**Caption**: "Test your analysis skills! 🎯"

### Story 9: Contractor Spotlight
**Format**: Partner feature showcase
**Interactive**: "Need a contractor?" poll
**Caption**: "Connect with verified pros! 🔨"

### Story 10: Weekly Recap
**Format**: Content summary and preview
**Interactive**: "What do you want to see?" question sticker
**Caption**: "Weekly dose of proptech wisdom! 📚"

## Story Strategy:
- Post 2-3 stories daily
- Peak times: 7-9 AM, 12-2 PM, 5-7 PM
- Always include interactive elements
- Create highlights for evergreen content
- Track completion rates and engagement
