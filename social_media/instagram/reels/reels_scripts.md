## Reels Production Guidelines:

### Technical Specifications:
- **Aspect Ratio**: 9:16 (vertical)
- **Resolution**: 1080x1920 minimum
- **Duration**: 15-60 seconds optimal
- **File Format**: MP4

### Visual Style:
- Brand colors throughout
- Professional, clean aesthetics
- Dynamic transitions
- Clear, readable text overlays
- Consistent logo placement

### Audio Strategy:
- Use trending sounds when appropriate
- Ensure audio quality is professional
- Consider voiceovers for complex explanations
- Keep background music at appropriate levels

### Engagement Optimization:
- Hook viewers in first 3 seconds
- Include clear calls-to-action
- Use trending hashtags
- Encourage comments and shares
- Cross-promote to other platforms

### Posting Strategy:
- **Frequency**: 2-3 Reels per week
- **Best Times**: Tuesday-Thursday, 11 AM-1 PM, 5-7 PM
- **Hashtag Mix**: 3-5 trending + 5-10 niche hashtags
- **Engagement**: Respond to comments within 2 hours
