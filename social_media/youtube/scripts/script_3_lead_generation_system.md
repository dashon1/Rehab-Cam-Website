## Main Content (45 seconds - 5:00 minutes)

### The Lead Generation Framework (45 seconds - 1:30 minutes)
"Most agents approach lead generation backwards. They focus on tactics instead of systems. Here's what works:

The 3-2-1 Rule:
- 3 prospecting activities daily
- 2 follow-up touches per lead
- 1 new lead source per month

Let's dive into each component..."

### Daily Prospecting Activities (1:30 - 2:45 minutes)
"Activity 1: Sphere of Influence (20 minutes)
- Call 10 people from your database
- Use the 'Hello, Hope You're Doing Well' script
- Ask for referrals to people who might be buying or selling

Activity 2: Expired Listings (20 minutes)
- Call 10 expired listings in your area
- Use the 'Better Market Analysis' approach
- Focus on what you can do differently than the previous agent

Activity 3: For Sale By Owners (20 minutes)
- Target FSBOs using the 'Partnership' angle
- Offer your expertise without being pushy
- Build relationships, not just pitch services"

### Follow-Up System (2:45 - 3:45 minutes)
"Here's the mistake that kills most agents: they follow up once or twice and give up. Here's the proven follow-up sequence:

Day 1: Initial contact call
Day 3: Text with market update
Day 7: Email with home valuation tool
Day 14: Social media connection and engagement
Day 30: Phone call with new listing opportunity
Day 60: Market report with relevant properties

The key is VALUE in every touch - never just 'checking in.'"

### Lead Source Strategy (3:45 - 4:30 minutes)
"One new lead source per month. Here are my top 5:

1. Facebook Ads (invest 5% of gross income)
2. Open House Visitor System
3. Referral Partner Network
4. Community Events and Networking
5. Sphere of Influence Campaigns

Pick ONE, master it, then add the next. Most agents try to do everything and succeed at nothing."

### Scripts That Convert (4:30 - 5:00 minutes)
"Here are three scripts that work:

Referral Request Script:
'Hi [Name], I'm working with a few people looking to buy/sell in [area]. Do you know anyone who might be interested in the current market? I'd love to help them like I've helped you.'

Expired Listing Script:
'Hi [Name], I noticed your home didn't sell. I've had great success helping sellers like yourself in this exact situation. I'd love to share what I would do differently.'

FSBO Script:
'Hi [Name], I work with a lot of For Sale By Owners and help them with the complex parts while they keep control of the sale. I'd love to share some insights that might be helpful.'"
