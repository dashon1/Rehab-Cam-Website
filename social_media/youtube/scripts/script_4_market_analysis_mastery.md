## Main Content (45 seconds - 6:00 minutes)

### Step 1: Demographic Pulse Check (45 seconds - 1:45 minutes)
"First, understand WHO is moving to your market:
- Population growth rate (target: 2%+ annually)
- Median age trends (look for 25-45 age groups)
- Income level changes (median household income growth)
- Education levels (college graduates percentage)

I use Census.gov data and pull a 5-year trend. If people with money aren't moving TO your area, don't invest there."

### Step 2: Employment Ecosystem (1:45 - 2:45 minutes)
"Jobs drive real estate. Analyze:
- Major employers in the area
- Job growth rates by industry
- Unemployment trends
- Commute patterns and transportation

Look for diverse employment sectors - one-employer towns are risky. Target markets with Fortune 500 companies or strong government/military presence."

### Step 3: Supply and Demand Indicators (2:45 - 3:45 minutes)
"This is where most people mess up. Check:
- Days on Market (DOM) - trending down = good
- Months of inventory (target: under 4 months)
- New construction permits vs. population growth
- Rent-to-price ratios

The golden rule: If it takes longer than 90 days to sell, you have a buyer's market. Under 30 days? Seller's market."

### Step 4: Price Movement Analysis (3:45 - 4:30 minutes)
"Track price trends over 1, 3, and 5 years:
- Median home price changes
- Price per square foot trends
- Appreciation rate consistency
- Price vs. national average comparisons

Target markets with 3-5% annual appreciation, but be wary of markets over 8% - they often crash hard."

### Step 5: Investment Fundamentals (4:30 - 5:15 minutes)
"For investors specifically:
- Rental yield potential (gross yield targets: 8%+)
- Vacancy rates (under 5% = healthy market)
- Landlord-tenant laws and regulations
- Property tax trends

Use BiggerPockets calculators and local MLS data for accurate numbers."

### Step 6: Risk Assessment Matrix (5:15 - 6:00 minutes)
"Finally, identify red flags:
- Overdevelopment (too many condos/apartments)
- Economic dependency (single major industry)
- Natural disaster risks
- Political/regulatory changes

Rate each factor 1-10, multiply by importance, and add up your score. Anything under 70/100 needs serious consideration."
