## Production Guidelines for Educational Content:

### Visual Standards:
- Use clear, readable fonts (minimum 24pt for mobile)
- High contrast text over background images
- Consistent brand colors throughout
- Simple graphics and icons to support points

### Engagement Tactics:
- Start with relatable pain points
- Use numbers and specific percentages
- Include "cheat sheet" takeaways
- End with actionable next steps

### Trust Building:
- Cite statistics when possible
- Use real examples (with permission)
- Acknowledge limitations and variations
- Provide disclaimers for legal/financial advice

### Content Extensions:
- Create downloadable guides for each tip
- Link to longer-form blog content
- Offer consultation for complex topics
- Build email list through lead magnets

### Call-to-Action Strategies:
- Ask for specific experiences
- Request questions for future content
- Invite followers to save/share
- Encourage consultation inquiries
