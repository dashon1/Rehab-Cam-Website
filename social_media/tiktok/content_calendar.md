# TikTok Content Calendar for Real Estate Professionals

## Content Calendar Overview
Consistent posting 4-5 times per week can improve engagement. This calendar provides a structured approach to maintain regular content while allowing for trending opportunities and spontaneous content.

## Weekly Content Framework

### Monday: Motivation Monday
**Content Type**: Educational/Market Update
**Format**: 30-60 second tip or market insight
**Theme**: Week ahead preparation, market trends
**Examples**:
- "3 things to watch in the market this week"
- "Monday motivation for first-time buyers"
- "Market Monday: Interest rate update"

### Tuesday: Tip Tuesday  
**Content Type**: Educational Quick Tips
**Format**: 15-30 second actionable advice
**Theme**: Specific buyer/seller tips
**Examples**:
- "Quick tip: How to read a property disclosure"
- "Red flags to check during showing"
- "Seller tip: Boost your home's curb appeal"

### Wednesday: Wednesday Wisdom
**Content Type**: Behind-the-scenes or educational
**Format**: 30-45 second process or expertise content
**Theme**: Agent expertise, market knowledge
**Examples**:
- "How agents really analyze comparables"
- "What happens during a home inspection"
- "Behind the scenes: Market analysis process"

### Thursday: Throwback Thursday (or Trend Thursday)
**Content Type**: Trending format or success story
**Format**: Adapt to trending audio/format
**Theme**: Relatable agent moments or client success
**Examples**:
- "Things I wish I knew my first year"
- "POV: Client wants everything under budget"
- "Success story Thursday: First-time buyers"

### Friday: Feature Friday
**Content Type**: Property showcase or community spotlight
**Format**: 30-60 second property tour or neighborhood highlight
**Theme**: Market inventory, local community
**Examples**:
- "Friday Feature: Stunning downtown condo"
- "Neighborhood spotlight: Best schools in [area]"
- "Weekend open house preview"

### Weekend: Special Content
**Content Type**: Lifestyle, personal, or trending
**Format**: 15-45 second authentic content
**Theme**: Work-life balance, trending participation
**Examples**:
- "Weekend market prep"
- "Client appreciation posts"
- "Trending audio with real estate twist"

## Daily Posting Schedule

### Optimal Posting Times (Local Market Dependent)
**Monday-Friday**: 
- 7:00-8:00 AM (morning commute)
- 12:00-1:00 PM (lunch break)
- 5:00-6:00 PM (evening commute)

**Weekend**:
- 10:00-11:00 AM (leisure browsing)
- 2:00-3:00 PM (afternoon scroll)
- 7:00-8:00 PM (evening unwind)

### Time Zone Considerations
- Adjust posting times for your target market
- Consider 2-3 posting windows for maximum reach
- Use TikTok Analytics to identify your audience's peak times

## Content Planning Template

### Weekly Planning Session (Sunday)
1. **Review Trending Content**: Check TikTok Creative Center for trending audio
2. **Plan 3-4 Core Videos**: Based on weekly theme
3. **Prepare Props/Content**: Gather materials needed
4. **Schedule Filming Time**: Plan 2-3 hour batch filming session

### Content Batch Creation
**Monthly Batch Production**:
- Film 15-20 videos in one 3-4 hour session
- Create content for each weekly theme
- Film multiple versions of trending formats
- Prepare seasonal/timely content

**Weekly Batch Production**:
- Sunday: Plan and prepare
- Monday: Film 3-4 videos
- Tuesday-Friday: Post daily content
- Weekend: Engage with audience, create spontaneous content

## Content Performance Tracking

### Weekly Analytics Review
**Key Metrics**:
- Total views and reach
- Engagement rate (likes, comments, shares)
- Profile visits and followers gained
- Comment quality and inquiries generated

### Monthly Strategy Adjustment
**Review Questions**:
- Which content types performed best?
- What trending formats worked for your market?
- Which posting times had highest engagement?
- What hashtags drove the most discovery?
- Are you hitting your engagement rate goals?

## Content Creation Resources

### Essential Tools
- **Video Editing**: InShot, CapCut, or TikTok native editor
- **Scheduling**: TikTok Creator Tools or third-party scheduler
- **Analytics**: TikTok Analytics, Creator Fund insights
- **Trending Discovery**: TikTok Creative Center, Exploding Topics

### Content Creation Tips
- Batch create content on weekends
- Use trending audio within 24-48 hours
- Film in vertical 9:16 format
- Keep videos 15-60 seconds for best performance
- Use clear, readable text overlays
- Include captions for accessibility

### Engagement Strategy
- Respond to comments within 2-4 hours
- Ask questions to encourage engagement
- Share user-generated content (with permission)
- Cross-promote on other social platforms
- Use comments for content inspiration

This content calendar provides structure while maintaining flexibility for trending opportunities and spontaneous content creation.
