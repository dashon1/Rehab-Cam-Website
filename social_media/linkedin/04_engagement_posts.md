## Engagement Strategy Notes

**Question Design Principles:**
- Multiple choice options to encourage participation
- Real-world scenarios that resonate with target audience
- Open-ended follow-up questions for deeper engagement
- Controversial or thought-provoking topics for discussion

**Engagement Optimization:**
- Ask questions that require real experience to answer
- Provide examples to spark additional comments
- Create scenarios that encourage story sharing
- Design polls that feel valuable to participate in

**Lead Generation Integration:**
- Include questions about current challenges and pain points
- Ask about technology adoption and willingness to try new tools
- Inquire about investment goals and growth plans
- Identify prospects for demo requests and consultations

**Community Building Focus:**
- Encourage peer-to-peer knowledge sharing
- Create opportunities for networking and connection
- Build community around shared challenges and solutions
- Establish Rehab-E-Cam as helpful resource and thought leader

**Follow-Up Strategy:**
- Respond to all comments within 2-4 hours
- Ask follow-up questions to commenters
- Share relevant insights and additional resources
- Direct serious prospects to private messages or demos
