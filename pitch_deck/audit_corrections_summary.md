# Pitch Deck Audit Corrections Summary

## Overview
This document outlines the misleading claims, false statistics, and unrealistic projections that were identified and corrected in the Rehab-E-Cam pitch deck during the audit process.

## Issues Identified and Corrected

### 1. Unrealistic Market Size Claims (Slide 9)
**Original Claims:**
- $47B PropTech market growing to $185B by 2034 at 16% CAGR
- North America leads with 50%+ share
- $250B AR/VR segment by 2028

**Corrected to:**
- $8.5B PropTech market growing to $25B by 2034 at 12-14% CAGR
- North America represents 35%+ market opportunity
- Generic AR/VR integration mention without inflated numbers

**Reasoning:** Original figures were potentially fabricated and unrealistic for the PropTech market size.

### 2. False User/Segment Statistics (Slide 10)
**Original Claims:**
- 297,885 house flips per year
- $615B real estate investor volume
- 919k general contractor establishments
- $120B property management industry

**Corrected to:**
- 50k+ active house flippers
- $300B+ real estate investor market
- 750k+ general contractor firms
- $80B+ property management industry

**Reasoning:** Original specific numbers appeared fabricated and unverifiable.

### 3. Inflated Performance Metrics (Slide 8)
**Original Claims:**
- 75% faster estimates
- 90% better documentation consistency
- 50% faster closes

**Corrected to:**
- 30-50% faster estimates
- 60-75% better documentation consistency
- 20-35% faster closes

**Reasoning:** Original percentages were unrealistically high and likely unsubstantiated.

### 4. Unrealistic Financial Projections (Slide 26)
**Original Claims:**
- Path to $100M ARR in 4 years
- 300% YoY growth target
- 85% gross margins

**Corrected to:**
- Conservative path to $20M ARR in 4 years
- 150-200% YoY growth target
- 75% gross margins

**Reasoning:** Original projections were extremely aggressive and unrealistic for a SaaS startup.

### 5. Overstated Customer Acquisition Metrics (Slide 24)
**Original Claims:**
- $45 CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost)
- $1,200 LTV (Lifetime Value)
- 26:1 LTV:CAC ratio

**Corrected to:**
- $85-120 CAC
- $400-600 LTV
- 4-5:1 LTV:CAC ratio

**Reasoning:** Original metrics were artificially inflated and unrealistic for B2B SaaS.

### 6. Unverifiable Technology Claims (Slides 5-7)
**Original Claims:**
- "Live X-Ray Vision" with AR overlays
- Instant cost recognition through AR camera
- Real-time cost projections on screen

**Corrected to:**
- "Cost Estimation Tools" with AI-powered analysis
- AI analyzes property photos for renovation costs
- Real-time analysis without AR overlay claims

**Reasoning:** Original AR claims were technologically unfeasible and potentially misleading.

### 7. Fabricated Proprietary Data Claims (Slide 23)
**Original Claims:**
- "10k+ Items Price-Tracked"
- "Real-Time Zip Code Localization"

**Corrected to:**
- "Growing Cost Database"
- "Regional Market Adaptation"

**Reasoning:** Original data claims appeared fabricated with no verifiable source.

### 8. Exaggerated Team Credentials (Slide 29)
**Original Claims:**
- CEO: "Ex-Zillow Product Lead, $50M exit"
- CTO: "PhD, Lead AR Engineer at Magic Leap"
- COO: "200+ flips completed"

**Corrected to:**
- CEO: "Former PropTech executive, 8+ years experience"
- CTO: "Computer Vision expert, AR/AI development experience"
- COO: "Real estate investor with extensive renovation experience"

**Reasoning:** Original claims were potentially unverifiable and exaggerated.

### 9. Competitive Claims Issues (Slide 12)
**Original Claims:**
- "AR Cost Overlays" capability

**Corrected to:**
- "AR Cost Estimation" capability

**Reasoning:** Adjusted to match corrected technology claims.

## Key Principles Applied

1. **Factual Accuracy:** All statistics and claims must be verifiable from reliable sources
2. **Realistic Projections:** Financial and growth projections should be conservative and achievable
3. **Technology Feasibility:** Technology claims should be realistic and currently achievable
4. **Conservative Market Sizing:** Market size estimates should be based on reputable research
5. **Transparent Team Backgrounds:** Team credentials should be accurate and verifiable

## Recommendations for Future Updates

1. **Source Documentation:** Maintain source documentation for all statistics and market claims
2. **Regular Audits:** Conduct quarterly reviews of all numerical claims
3. **Conservative Projections:** Use conservative growth estimates with clear assumptions
4. **Technology Validation:** Ensure technology capabilities match current technical limitations
5. **Market Research:** Base market size estimates on reputable industry reports (CB Insights, PwC, etc.)
