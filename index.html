Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate are the AR measurements?

Rehab-E-Cam uses LiDAR technology (available on iPhone 12 Pro and newer, and iPad Pro) to achieve 98-99% accuracy for measurements. For older devices, we use advanced computer vision with 95%+ accuracy.

Does it integrate with Procore or QuickBooks?

Yes! Our Portfolio plan supports native integrations with Procore, Autodesk Construction Cloud, and QuickBooks Online. You can push SOWs and budgets directly to your management software.

Where does the pricing data come from?

We aggregate real-time pricing from major suppliers like Home Depot and Lowe's, adjusted for your specific zip code. Labor rates are updated weekly based on local market averages.

Can I export reports for my lender?

Absolutely. Rehab-E-Cam generates professional, lender-compliant PDF packets that include photos, line-item budgets, and project timelines. Our users report 100% lender acceptance.
