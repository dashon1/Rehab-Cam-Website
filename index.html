Simple Setup

Up and Running in Under 10 Minutes

No specialized equipment required. Just your smartphone or laptop camera and an internet connection.

1

Your Therapist Sets Your Plan

Your physical therapist prescribes your home exercise program inside RehabCam — with video demonstrations, rep counts, and form cues tailored to your condition.

2

You Exercise — Camera Watches Your Form

Open RehabCam on your phone or laptop, start your session, and follow the on-screen prompts. The AI tracks your joints in real time and alerts you to form errors before they cause harm.

3

AI Logs Every Rep and Metric

Range of motion, rep quality, completion rate, pain ratings, and session notes are automatically logged — no paper journals, no guesswork.

4

Therapist Reviews Data Between Visits

Your PT gets a full dashboard of your between-appointment activity. They can message you adjustments, flag concerns, and update your plan without a full visit.

Platform Features

Everything You Need for a Full Recovery

Built for patients and clinicians alike — every feature designed to close the gap between clinic visits.

🌌

Live Session Monitoring

Conduct live video sessions with your therapist watching in real time. Get instant verbal feedback on your form just like an in-clinic appointment — from your living room.

AI Form Analysis

Computer vision tracks 33 body landmarks per frame. The system detects compensatory movements, limited range of motion, and unsafe loading patterns before they become setbacks.

Progress Tracking Dashboard

Visual charts of your range of motion, strength assessments, and functional scores over time. See your recovery in data — and stay motivated with clear milestones.

📋

Exercise Form Feedback

Each exercise includes video demonstration, target range markers, and real-time posture overlay. A score is given for every rep so you know exactly how you're doing.

Smart Reminders & Adherence
