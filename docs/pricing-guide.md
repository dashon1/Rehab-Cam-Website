## What's Included at Each Tier

### Basic — $49/month

Designed for motivated patients who want guided home exercise sessions with AI feedback and don't yet have a therapist on the platform.

**Included:**
- Unlimited AI-guided exercise sessions
- AI pose analysis and per-rep form scoring on all exercises
- Access to 200+ exercise library organized by body region and condition
- Progress dashboard with range of motion, adherence, and form trends
- Session history and export (90-day rolling window)
- Patient-reported pain and effort tracking
- Standard exercise programs by condition (knee, shoulder, hip, back, etc.)
- iOS app, Android app, and web access
- Email and live chat support (response within 24 business hours)

**Not included in Basic:**
- Therapist portal access (your PT cannot log in to view your data)
- Live video sessions with a therapist
- Custom program prescription
- Secure therapist-patient messaging
- Insurance-formatted session notes
- Unlimited session history

**Basic is right for you if:**
- You have a prescribed exercise program and want AI guidance doing it correctly
- Your PT is not on RehabCam but you want to self-track your progress
- You are doing general maintenance/prevention rather than acute rehab

**Pro is right for you if:**
- You are post-surgery or managing a significant injury with a therapist

- Your insurance may cover telehealth PT and you need documentation
- You want a collaborative care model rather than working alone

## Billing FAQ

**When am I charged?**
Monthly billing charges on the same calendar date each month (e.g., if you start on the 15th, you are billed on the 15th each month). Annual billing charges once upfront.

**What payment methods are accepted?**
Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, and HSA/FSA debit cards. ACH bank transfer available for Clinic annual plans (contact sales).

**Can I change plans mid-cycle?**
Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle.

**What happens if I cancel?**
You retain access until the end of your current billing period. No partial-month refunds on monthly plans. Annual plans cancelled within 30 days receive a prorated refund for unused months.

**Is there a setup fee?**
No setup fees on any plan.

**Can I pause my subscription?**
Yes. You can pause billing for up to 3 months (once per 12-month period). Your data is preserved during the pause.

**Do therapists pay separately?**
No. On the Pro plan, the patient pays the subscription and the therapist gets portal access at no additional charge. On the Clinic plan, the clinic pays and all therapists at the practice have access.

**Is there a discount for non-profits or academic institutions?**
Yes — 30% discount for verified non-profits and academic PT programs. Contact sales@rehabcam.com with your organization's documentation.

## Insurance and HSA/FSA

**HSA/FSA Eligibility:**
RehabCam subscriptions are eligible for payment with HSA (Health Savings Account) and FSA (Flexible Spending Account) debit cards when used as part of a prescribed rehabilitation program. Keep your prescription documentation for reimbursement purposes.

**Insurance Reimbursement:**
Telehealth physical therapy coverage varies by insurance plan. The Pro and Clinic plans generate documentation using the following CPT codes to support reimbursement claims:

- **97110** — Therapeutic procedure (exercise)
- **97530** — Therapeutic activities
- **98967** — Telephone assessment and management (telehealth)
- **G0151** — Services by a qualified physical therapist (Medicare Advantage, select plans)

We recommend verifying your specific coverage with your insurer before relying on reimbursement. Our support team can provide a benefit verification checklist and a letter of medical necessity template.
