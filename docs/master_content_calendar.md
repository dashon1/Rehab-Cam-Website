# Rehab-E-Cam Master Content Calendar
## Integrated Marketing Strategy for Real Estate Technology Platform

---

## Executive Summary

This master content calendar synchronizes all marketing activities across channels to drive prospects through the complete funnel. By integrating blog content, social media, video releases, email campaigns, PR opportunities, and event marketing, we create a cohesive experience that builds awareness, drives consideration, and accelerates conversion across our four target segments: real estate investors, house flippers, contractors, and property managers.

**Campaign Theme for 2025:** "Field Evidence Revolution" - Positioning Rehab-E-Cam as the essential field OS that transforms property evaluation through AR-powered documentation.

**Primary Objectives:**
- Drive qualified leads across all target segments
- Establish thought leadership in real estate technology
- Increase trial signups and customer acquisition
- Build brand awareness and market presence
- Support sales enablement and conversion

## Content Distribution Strategy

### Multi-Channel Approach

**Blog Content (Primary Hub)**
- Published bi-weekly (24 posts annually)
- SEO optimized for target keywords
- Integrated with email campaigns
- Repurposed for social media

**Social Media Strategy**
- LinkedIn: Professional content for B2B segments (5 posts/week)
- Instagram: Visual content and stories (7 posts/week)
- TikTok: Short-form video content (3 posts/week)
- Twitter/X: Industry news and thought leadership (7 posts/week)

**Video Content (5 Core Videos)**
- YouTube: Long-form tutorials and demos (weekly)
- Instagram Reels: Short-form highlights (3/week)
- TikTok: Trending format adaptations (3/week)
- LinkedIn: Professional demonstrations (1/week)

**Email Campaigns**
- Weekly newsletter (industry insights and company updates)
- Segment-specific nurture sequences (5-email series)
- Monthly market analysis reports
- Quarterly webinar invitations

**PR Strategy**
- Monthly press releases (product updates, milestones)
- Quarterly industry reports
- Guest articles in trade publications
- Speaking opportunities at conferences

**Event Marketing**
- Quarterly webinars
- Annual conference participation (3-4 major events)
- Monthly virtual demos
- Quarterly user group meetings

## Cross-Promotion Workflow

### Content Creation Pipeline
1. **Blog Post Creation** (Week 1)
2. **Social Media Adaptations** (Week 1-2)
   - LinkedIn article adaptation
   - Instagram carousel creation
   - TikTok video script
   - Twitter thread summary
3. **Video Content Integration** (Week 2)
   - Embed videos in blog posts
   - Create video clips for social media
   - Include in email campaigns
4. **Email Campaign Launch** (Week 2-3)
   - Blog post highlights
   - Video content inclusion
   - CTA alignment with blog content
5. **PR and Outreach** (Week 3-4)
   - Industry publication pitches
   - Media interview opportunities
   - Conference presentation proposals

### Funnel Alignment

**Awareness Stage (Top of Funnel)**
- Blog posts targeting industry trends
- Social media thought leadership
- PR placements in trade publications
- Video content showcasing technology

**Consideration Stage (Middle of Funnel)**
- Detailed feature demonstrations
- Case studies and success stories
- Comparison guides and ROI calculators
- Webinar content and demos

**Decision Stage (Bottom of Funnel)**
- Free trial promotions
- Consultation booking CTAs
- Customer testimonials
- Limited-time offers

### Traffic and Engagement Metrics
- Blog traffic growth (target: 25% month-over-month)
- Social media engagement rate (target: >5%)
- Video view-through rate (target: >70%)
- Email open rate (target: >25%)
- Email click-through rate (target: >5%)

### Lead Generation Metrics
- Lead generation by content type
- Trial signups from content traffic (target: 100/month)
- Consultation bookings (target: 20/month)
- Demo requests (target: 50/month)

### Conversion Metrics
- Content-to-trial conversion rate (target: 3-5%)

- Lifetime value by acquisition source

### Brand Metrics
- Brand mention sentiment
- Share of voice in industry
- Website direct traffic growth
- Branded search volume increase

The calendar is designed to be:

Regular monitoring and optimization ensure that the content strategy evolves with market conditions and customer needs, maintaining effectiveness throughout 2025 and beyond.
