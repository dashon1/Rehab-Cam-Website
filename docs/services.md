# RehabCam — Service Descriptions

## What Is RehabCam?

RehabCam is a telehealth physical therapy monitoring platform that bridges the gap between in-clinic appointments. Using your smartphone or laptop camera, RehabCam captures your exercise sessions, analyzes movement quality with AI pose estimation, and gives your physical therapist clinical-grade data about your recovery progress — without requiring you to leave home.

RehabCam is not a replacement for physical therapy. It is a tool that extends the reach and effectiveness of your existing PT relationship.

---

## Core Services

### 1. AI-Guided Home Exercise Sessions

**What it is:**
Patients follow their prescribed exercise program through the RehabCam app. The camera tracks joint angles and movement patterns in real time using computer vision. A visual overlay shows the target range of motion, and a per-rep score indicates form quality.

**How it works:**
- Patient opens the app and selects their prescribed session
- The app guides proper device placement (full body in frame)
- Each exercise plays with a video demonstration and verbal cue prompts
- AI tracks 33 body landmarks at 30fps and scores form on each rep
- Alerts appear on-screen when deviation from target form is detected (e.g., "knee tracking inward — focus on keeping knee over second toe")
- Session is logged automatically with timestamp, reps completed, form scores, and any flagged movements

**Who this is for:**
Post-surgical patients (knee, hip, shoulder, spine), chronic pain management, athletes in structured return-to-sport protocols, and anyone prescribed home exercise by a licensed PT.

---

### 2. Live Session Monitoring

**What it is:**
Scheduled video sessions where your physical therapist watches you complete exercises in real time and provides verbal coaching — just like an in-clinic appointment, conducted over a secure video connection.

**How it works:**
- Patient and therapist schedule a live session through the platform
- Both connect via encrypted video link (no third-party apps required)
- Therapist views patient's camera feed alongside AI form data overlay
- Therapist can pause, cue, and adjust exercises in real time
- Session is recorded (with patient consent) and stored in the patient file
- A clinical note is auto-generated after each live session

**Who this is for:**
Patients on the Pro and Clinic plans. Recommended frequency varies by protocol — typically 1–2 live sessions per week for acute post-surgical patients, reducing to monthly check-ins as recovery progresses.

---

### 3. Progress Tracking and Reporting

**What it is:**
A visual dashboard showing recovery metrics over time — range of motion measurements, session adherence rates, form quality trends, pain ratings, and functional scores.

**How it works:**
- After each session, metrics are logged and visualized on the dashboard
- Therapists and patients each have a view of the dashboard calibrated to their role
- Milestone markers (e.g., "90-degree knee flexion achieved") are tracked against protocol benchmarks
- Reports can be exported as PDF for insurance, workers' comp, or referring physician documentation

**Key metrics tracked:**
- Joint range of motion (degrees) over time
- Session completion rate (adherence %)
- Per-exercise form scores (0–100)
- Patient-reported pain level (0–10 scale per session)
- Time-to-milestone vs. protocol average

---

### 4. Custom Program Prescription (Pro and Clinic Plans)

**What it is:**
Physical therapists use the RehabCam therapist portal to build and assign fully custom exercise programs — specifying exercises, sets, reps, rest periods, frequency, and progression criteria for each patient.

**How it works:**
- Therapist logs into the portal and selects a patient
- Builds a program from the 200+ exercise library or uploads custom exercise videos
- Sets progression rules (e.g., "advance to phase 2 when 3 consecutive sessions score above 80")
- Program is pushed to the patient's app and available immediately
- Therapist can update the program between sessions without a live call

**Exercise library includes:**
Lower extremity (knee, hip, ankle), upper extremity (shoulder, elbow, wrist), spine and core, balance and proprioception, and functional movement patterns.

---

### 5. Secure Therapist-Patient Messaging

**What it is:**
HIPAA-compliant direct messaging between patients and their assigned physical therapist — for questions, form check-ins, and session feedback outside of scheduled appointments.

**How it works:**
- Built-in encrypted messaging within the app (no external apps needed)
- Patients can attach a short video clip directly from a session for therapist review
- Therapists can respond with text, voice notes, or video response
- All messages are logged to the patient's clinical record

---

### 6. Insurance Documentation Support

**What it is:**
Automated generation of structured session notes and progress reports formatted to support insurance reimbursement claims for telehealth physical therapy services.

**Documents generated:**
- Per-session clinical notes (date, exercises performed, reps/sets, form scores, patient-reported pain)
- Monthly progress summaries with measurable functional outcomes
- Adherence reports showing completion rates
- Range of motion progression charts with timestamps

## Who RehabCam Serves

### Individual Patients
Anyone recovering from injury, surgery, or managing a chronic musculoskeletal condition who has been prescribed a home exercise program by a licensed physical therapist. Particularly valuable for patients who cannot attend frequent in-clinic visits due to distance, transportation, work schedule, or cost.

### Physical Therapists in Private Practice
Solo PTs and small group practices who want to extend their care model beyond appointment hours. RehabCam allows a single therapist to actively monitor more patients between visits without proportionally increasing their schedule.

### Physical Therapy Clinics
Multi-provider clinics that want a standardized platform for home exercise program delivery. The Clinic plan's multi-patient dashboard allows clinic directors to see at-a-glance which patients are adherent, which are struggling, and where intervention is needed.

### Sports Medicine Programs
Athletic trainers and sports medicine physicians managing return-to-sport protocols for athletes. RehabCam's data-driven approach to functional milestones supports clearance decisions with objective evidence.

## Setup Process

### For Patients

1. **Sign up** at rehabcam.com — choose a plan, start free trial
2. **Connect with your therapist** — enter their RehabCam therapist ID (if they use the platform) or start with AI-guided programs
3. **Download the app** — iOS App Store or Google Play (web app also available)
4. **Set up your exercise space** — the app walks you through camera placement for your first session
5. **Start your first session** — follow on-screen prompts and complete your prescribed exercises

**Time to first session:** Under 15 minutes from signup.

### For Physical Therapists (Pro/Clinic Plans)

1. **Create a therapist account** at rehabcam.com/therapists
2. **Invite your patients** — send an email invitation from the portal
3. **Build your first program** — use the program builder or select from protocol templates
4. **Review your first session data** — patients appear on your dashboard after their first session

**Onboarding support:** Clinic plan includes a dedicated onboarding call with a RehabCam implementation specialist.
