This comprehensive social media strategy positions Rehab-E-Cam as the leading AR-powered field solution for real estate investors, house flippers, contractors, and property managers. Through platform-specific content strategies, strategic influencer partnerships, and data-driven community building, we'll establish thought leadership while driving qualified leads and app adoption across all major social platforms.

- Generate 5,000+ qualified leads and 1,000+ app trials in 90 days

```
"Hi [Name], thank you for bringing this to our attention. We take product quality seriously and are investigating this issue immediately. Our team will provide an update within 24 hours. In the meantime, please contact our support team at [contact] for immediate assistance."
```

**Customer Complaint Response**:
```
"We sincerely apologize for your experience. Your feedback is valuable to us and helps us improve. We've forwarded your concern to our customer success team who will reach out within 4 hours to resolve this matter. Thank you for giving us the opportunity to make this right."
```

**Industry Issue Response**:
```
"We're monitoring the recent developments in [industry issue] and want to assure our community that [company response/position]. We're committed to [specific action] and will provide updates as available. Please reach out with any questions."
```

### Reputation Management Strategy

#### Positive Reputation Building:
1. **Customer Success Stories**: Regular feature of satisfied customers
2. **Industry Recognition**: Participation in awards and recognition programs
3. **Thought Leadership**: Consistent sharing of industry insights and expertise
4. **Community Support**: Active participation in industry discussions and events
5. **Transparency**: Open communication about challenges and improvements

#### Reputation Recovery Plan:

**Phase 1: Immediate Response (0-4 hours)**
- Acknowledge the issue publicly
- Provide initial explanation or investigation timeline
- Offer direct contact for affected parties
- Monitor all channels for additional feedback

**Phase 2: Investigation & Communication (4-24 hours)**
- Conduct thorough investigation
- Develop comprehensive response plan
- Communicate findings to stakeholders
- Implement immediate corrective measures

**Phase 3: Resolution & Follow-up (24-72 hours)**
- Implement permanent solutions
- Communicate resolution to community
- Follow up with affected parties
- Document lessons learned

**Phase 4: Prevention & Monitoring (Ongoing)**
- Implement preventive measures
- Enhance monitoring systems
- Regular reputation health checks
- Community feedback integration
