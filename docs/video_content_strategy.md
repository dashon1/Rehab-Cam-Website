# Rehab-E-Cam Video Content Strategy
## 5 Mini Videos for Social Media

### Executive Summary

This comprehensive video content strategy outlines 5 mini videos designed to showcase Rehab-E-Cam's unique value proposition for real estate investors, house flippers, contractors, and property managers. Each video is optimized for different stages of the customer journey and various social media platforms, with a focus on demonstrating the platform's signature "Live X-Ray" feature and integrated workflow.

**Target Audience:** Real estate investors, house flippers, general contractors, property managers  
**Primary Goal:** Drive app downloads and trial signups  
**Secondary Goals:** Build brand awareness, establish thought leadership, generate qualified leads

### Production Guidelines

**Equipment:**
- iPhone 14 Pro or equivalent for high-quality footage
- Ring light for consistent lighting
- Wireless lavalier microphone for clear audio
- Smartphone gimbal for smooth movement

**Filming Locations:**
- Actual properties (with permission)
- Professional office settings
- On-site construction/rehab locations

**Post-Production:**
- Adobe Premiere Pro or Final Cut Pro
- Consistent brand color grading
- Professional motion graphics
- Platform-specific aspect ratios

**Talent:**

- Authentic, relatable presentation style
