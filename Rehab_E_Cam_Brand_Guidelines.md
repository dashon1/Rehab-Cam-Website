# Rehab-E-Cam Brand Guidelines

## Brand Overview

Rehab-E-Cam is a revolutionary AR-powered real estate investment platform that transforms how investors find, fund, and flip properties. Our brand reflects innovation, trust, and professional expertise in the PropTech industry.

## Brand Positioning

**For real estate investors who need to make fast, accurate, and profitable decisions, Rehab-E-Cam is the all-in-one mobile platform that combines AR-powered scoping, automated estimation, and investor-grade deal analysis.**

## Core Brand Values

- **Innovation**: Cutting-edge AR technology and AI-powered analysis
- **Trust**: Professional, reliable tools for serious investors
- **Efficiency**: Streamlined workflows that save time and reduce errors
- **Accuracy**: Data-driven insights for confident decision-making
- **Accessibility**: User-friendly design for field and office use

## Logo System

### Primary Logo
- **Usage**: Main brand representation for pitch decks, presentations, and primary marketing materials
- **File**: `brand_primary_logo.png`
- **Minimum size**: 120px width for digital, 1 inch for print

### Horizontal Logo
- **Usage**: Headers, business cards, letterhead, and horizontal layouts
- **File**: `brand_horizontal_logo.png`
- **Minimum size**: 150px width for digital, 1.5 inches for print

### Stacked Logo
- **Usage**: Social media profiles, app icons, square formats, and compact spaces
- **File**: `brand_stacked_logo.png`
- **Minimum size**: 80px width for digital, 0.75 inches for print

## Color Palette

### Primary Colors
- **Royal Blue**: #3B82F6
  - Usage: Primary actions, links, key highlights
  - Represents: Trust, professionalism, technology

- **Bright Green**: #22C55E
  - Usage: Success states, positive metrics, call-to-action accents
  - Represents: Growth, profit, success

### Secondary Colors
- **Dark Navy**: #0F172A
  - Usage: Headers, key statistics, premium features
  - Represents: Authority, sophistication, stability

- **Light Grey**: #F3F4F6
  - Usage: Backgrounds, subtle borders, inactive states
  - Represents: Cleanliness, simplicity, neutrality

- **White**: #FFFFFF
  - Usage: Primary background, text on dark backgrounds
  - Represents: Clarity, space, cleanliness

## Typography

### Primary Typeface
- **Recommended**: Inter, Roboto, or similar modern sans-serif
- **Usage**: All brand communications, digital interfaces, marketing materials

### Font Hierarchy
- **Headlines**: Bold weight (700), 24-48px
- **Subheadings**: Semi-bold weight (600), 18-24px
- **Body Text**: Regular weight (400), 14-16px
- **Captions**: Regular weight (400), 12-14px

## Iconography

### Feature Icons
- **Style**: Minimal line icons with consistent stroke weight
- **Usage**: App interfaces, feature highlights, presentations

## Application Examples

### Digital Applications
- **Website Headers**: Use horizontal logo with royal blue navigation
- **Mobile App**: Stacked logo for app icon and splash screens
- **Social Media**: Stacked logo for profile images, templates for posts
- **Email Signatures**: Horizontal logo with contact information

### Print Applications
- **Business Cards**: Horizontal logo with clean, professional layout
- **Letterhead**: Horizontal logo at top with company details
- **Trade Show Materials**: Large format banners with horizontal logo
- **Presentation Materials**: Brand guidelines cover and slide templates

### Marketing Materials
- **Pitch Decks**: Primary logo on cover, horizontal for content slides
- **Product Showcases**: Feature icons and Live X-Ray demonstrations
- **Case Studies**: Professional templates with logo placement
- **Infographics**: Color palette and typography for data visualization

## Brand Voice & Tone

### Voice Characteristics
- **Professional**: Expert-level knowledge without jargon
- **Confident**: Assertive about technology and results
- **Supportive**: Helpful and understanding of user challenges
- **Innovative**: Excited about technology and possibilities

### Tone Variations
- **Investor Communications**: Authoritative and data-driven
- **Product Features**: Enthusiastic about capabilities
- **Customer Support**: Patient and solution-focused
- **Marketing**: Compelling and benefit-focused

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do's
✓ Use approved logo variations in correct proportions
✓ Maintain consistent color palette across all materials
✓ Use high-resolution files for print materials
✓ Ensure adequate white space around logos
✓ Use approved typography hierarchy

### Don'ts
✗ Don't alter logo proportions or colors
✗ Don't use low-resolution images
✗ Don't place logos on busy backgrounds without proper contrast
✗ Don't mix with unauthorized fonts or colors
✗ Don't use outdated or inconsistent branding

## Implementation Guidelines

### Website Implementation
1. Use horizontal logo in header navigation
2. Apply royal blue for primary actions and links
3. Implement card-based layout matching brand aesthetic
4. Use Live X-Ray feature highlights with green accents

### Mobile App Implementation
1. Use stacked logo for app icon and splash screen
2. Apply brand colors for navigation and key actions
3. Implement clean, card-based interface design
4. Feature AR camera functionality prominently

### Marketing Materials
1. Maintain consistent logo placement and sizing
2. Use brand color palette for visual hierarchy
3. Include feature icons to highlight capabilities
4. Apply professional photography and mockups
